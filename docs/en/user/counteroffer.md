# Counteroffer

Respond to an offer with a counteroffer or accept/reject as buyer or seller.

## As seller: send counteroffer

1. **Receive the offer**  
   Notification by email and in the app. Review in "Offers received" or on the property card: price, payment method, term, conditions, buyer profile.

2. **Choose Counteroffer**  
   Instead of Accept or Reject, use **Counteroffer**.

3. **Adjust terms**  
   Change price, conditions, or deadlines as you wish. Create the counteroffer with the new terms.

4. **Send**  
   The buyer receives a notification and can accept, reject, or counter again.

## As buyer: respond to a counteroffer

1. **Receive the counteroffer**  
   Notification with the new terms proposed by the seller.

2. **Decide**  
   - **Accept**: The deal is closed. The legal process starts (case, lawyer, documents).
   - **Reject**: The negotiation for that offer ends.
   - **New counteroffer**: Send another proposal. The seller evaluates again.

## Negotiation cycle

The cycle can repeat until agreement or rejection. Each counteroffer is recorded in the offer history.
