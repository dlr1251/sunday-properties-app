# Contact a lawyer

How to communicate with the lawyer assigned to your case.

## When a lawyer is assigned

When an **offer or counteroffer is accepted**, a legal case is created and a lawyer is assigned. Buyer, seller, and lawyer receive a notification and can access the case.

## How to contact

1. **Open the case**  
   From the dashboard or "My negotiations", open the case linked to the accepted offer.

2. **Case chat**  
   On the case page there is a **chat** or messaging section. There you can write to the lawyer and the other party (depending on configuration). Use the chat for questions, coordination, and updates.

3. **Notifications**  
   You will receive notifications when the lawyer uploads documents, requests review or signature, or sends messages.

4. **Documents**  
   Review the case documents section. The lawyer uploads contracts, letters of intent, etc. If you have questions about a document, ask in the chat.

## Result

Communication centralized in the case: chat, documents, and notifications to follow the process until closing.
