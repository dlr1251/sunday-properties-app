# Terms and conditions

Summary of the platform's terms of use. The full, binding text is in the official legal document.

## Scope

Sunday Properties is a platform for real-estate transactions. The terms define the rights and obligations of users (owners, buyers, agents, lawyers, administrators).

## Principles

- **Transparency**: Disclosure of information relevant to transactions.
- **Security**: Handling of documents, analyses, and communications according to applicable standards.
- **Neutrality**: The platform acts as a technical intermediary; it does not assume responsibility for commercial agreements between parties.

## Roles and responsibilities

- **Owners and buyers**: Responsible for the accuracy of information, disclosure of relevant terms, and compliance with agreements.
- **Agents**: Responsible intermediaries; must verify information for listed properties.
- **Lawyers**: Legal review of documents and regulatory compliance.
- **Administrators**: Technical operation and content review; not responsible for commercial terms or agreements between parties.

## User obligations

- Provide complete, accurate, and up-to-date information.
- Disclose commercial terms, payment conditions, and relevant limitations.
- Use the platform appropriately and in line with applicable law.
