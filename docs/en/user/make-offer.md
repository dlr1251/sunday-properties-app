# Make an offer

How to submit a purchase offer on a property.

## Prerequisites

- Verified profile.
- Having visited the property (recommended) before offering.

## Steps

### 1. Open the form

On the property page, use **Make an offer** or the negotiation section. The offer form opens.

### 2. Enter details

- **Price**: Amount in COP. The system validates against sale price, minimum price, and negotiation rules. Visual indicators show whether the offer is in range, at the limit, or below.
- **Payment method**: Cash, financing, crypto, or mixed. If financing, indicate down payment, term, and lender if applicable.
- **Closing date**: Target closing date. Must respect the maximum period set by the seller; otherwise the offer may be rejected.
- **Conditions**: Additional conditions (text or structured). Optional.

### 3. Review and send

Review the automatic validation and system messages. Send the offer. It goes to "pending". The seller receives a notification.

## After sending

The seller can **accept**, **reject**, or send a **counteroffer**. If they send a counteroffer, you will be notified and can accept, reject, or counter again.
