# Register

How to create an account on Sunday Properties.

## Steps

1. **Go to Register**  
   On the home page, click "Sign up" or "Create account".

2. **Enter details**  
   - Email address  
   - Password (meet security requirements)  
   - Full name  
   - Phone (optional but recommended)  
   - User type (buyer, seller, agent, etc.) if requested

3. **Accept terms**  
   Accept the terms and conditions and privacy policy.

4. **Confirm email**  
   Check your inbox and click the confirmation link sent by the platform.

5. **Sign in**  
   Once confirmed, sign in with your email and password.

## After registering

- Complete your **profile** (photo, contact details).
- Request **identity verification** to be able to schedule visits, make offers, and publish properties.
