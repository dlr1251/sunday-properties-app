# Sign documents

Review and sign legal documents after accepting an offer.

## When it applies

When an **offer or counteroffer is accepted**, the legal process starts: a case is created, a lawyer is assigned, and documents (contracts, letters of intent, etc.) are generated. As buyer or seller, you will need to review and sign as instructed by the lawyer.

## Steps

1. **Notification**  
   You will receive notifications when there are documents pending review or signature.

2. **Open the case**  
   From the dashboard or "My negotiations", open the case linked to the accepted offer.

3. **Review documents**  
   The lawyer uploads or generates documents. Review each in the case documents section.

4. **Sign**  
   When a document is ready for signature, follow the on-screen instructions (e-signature, acceptance of terms, etc.).

5. **Case chat**  
   Use the case chat for questions and coordination with the lawyer and the other party.

## Result

Documents generated, reviewed, and signed as applicable. Traceability in notifications and case history.
