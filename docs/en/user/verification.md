# Identity verification

Why it's required and how to complete it.

## Why verify?

Identity verification is **required** to:

- Schedule property visits
- Make purchase offers
- Publish properties for sale

Without verification you cannot access these flows on the platform.

## Process

1. **Open the verification flow**  
   From your profile or dashboard, go to "Verify identity" or "Complete verification".

2. **Documents**  
   Upload a photo or copy of your ID (national ID, passport) as indicated by the platform.

3. **Selfie**  
   Some flows ask for a selfie to compare with the document.

4. **Review**  
   The team reviews the information. You will receive a notification when your profile is "verified".

## Result

Once verified, you can schedule visits, make offers, and publish properties according to your role.
