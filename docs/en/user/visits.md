# Schedule visits

Schedule visits as a buyer and manage requests as a seller.

## Prerequisites

- Verified profile (required to schedule).
- Published property with availability set (to schedule).

---

## For buyers: schedule a visit

### 1. Find the property

Search the catalog, filter by location, price, type, bedrooms, area. Open the listing and confirm it is published and has visits available.

### 2. Open the scheduling modal

On the property card, use "Schedule visit". The modal opens with calendar and time slots.

### 3. Choose date and time

- Browse months. Only available dates can be selected (based on availability and blocked dates).
- Typical rules: minimum advance (e.g. 24 h), not same day or past dates.
- After choosing a date, pick a time from the offered slots.

### 4. Accept NDA and pay

- Read and accept the confidentiality agreement (NDA). Required.
- Pay for the visit (e.g. 49,000 COP) via the available method (card, etc.).

### 5. Confirmation

You receive confirmation by email and in the platform. The visit appears in "My visits" with status "pending" until the seller confirms.

### 6. Attend and feedback

Show up at the agreed date and time. After the visit, you can submit feedback and rating.

---

## For sellers: manage requests

### 1. Receive the request

You get a notification by email and in the app. It includes: property, buyer, requested date and time, contact details.

### 2. Review in the dashboard

Requests appear in "Visits" or "Pending visits". Review the buyer's profile, verification, and that the date/time is within configured availability.

### 3. Decide

- **Confirm**: Accept date and time. The buyer receives confirmation.
- **Reject**: Optionally state a reason. The buyer receives a notification.
- **Reschedule**: Propose another date or time. The buyer can accept or reject.

### 4. Coordination and completion

After confirming, coordinate logistics (address, access). After the visit, add internal notes if needed. The visitor can leave feedback and rating.
