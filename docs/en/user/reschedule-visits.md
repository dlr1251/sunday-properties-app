# Reschedule or cancel visits

How to change the date/time of a visit or cancel it.

## As buyer

### Reschedule

1. Go to **My visits** (or the visits section in your dashboard).
2. Find the visit you want to change.
3. If the status allows it, use **Reschedule** or **Change date**.
4. Choose a new date and time from those available for that property.
5. The seller will receive the new request and can confirm or reject.

### Cancel

1. In **My visits**, open the visit you want to cancel.
2. Use **Cancel visit**.
3. Depending on platform policy, there may be a partial or full refund. Check the terms when cancelling.

## As seller

### Reschedule

1. In **Visit requests** or the property card, find the visit.
2. Instead of confirming, use **Reschedule** or **Propose another date**.
3. Enter the new proposed date and time.
4. The buyer will receive the proposal and can accept or reject.

### Reject

1. Review the request and, if you cannot accommodate it, use **Reject**.
2. Optionally give a reason. The buyer will receive a notification.
