# Privacy

Handling of personal data and use of cookies on Sunday Properties.

## Overview

The platform collects and processes data needed for registration, identity verification, property and visit management, offers, and the legal process. Data is used in accordance with the applicable privacy policy and regulations (e.g. personal data protection in Colombia).

## Data collected

- **Account and profile**: Email, name, phone, role, ID when applicable.
- **Properties and transactions**: Listing data, offers, visits, and legal cases.
- **Usage**: Logs, audit, and operational metrics when relevant.

## Use and retention

Data is used to operate the platform, meet legal obligations, and improve the service. Retention periods are defined in the official privacy policy.

## Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for session, preferences, and analytics. The application should inform users and, where required, obtain consent.

## Full document

For the complete privacy policy, see the official document published by Sunday Properties (in the app or at the indicated path).
