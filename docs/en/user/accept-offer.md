# Accept an offer

How to accept a purchase offer as seller.

## Prerequisites

- Be the owner or agent of the property.
- Have a received offer in "pending" status.

## Steps

1. **Receive and review the offer**  
   Notification by email and in the app. Review in "Offers received" or on the property card: price, payment method, term, conditions, buyer profile.

2. **Decide**  
   You can **Accept**, **Reject**, or send a **Counteroffer**. To accept:

3. **Accept**  
   If the offer and conditions are acceptable, use **Accept offer**. The legal process starts: a lawyer is assigned and the case is created. All parties receive a notification.

4. **After accepting**  
   A legal case is created linked to the offer, property, buyer, and seller. You can communicate with the lawyer and the other party via the case chat and review/sign documents as instructed by the lawyer.

## Result

Negotiation closed by acceptance. The flow continues in the legal process until signature and closing.
