## How the rental process works

1. Browse published properties with filters by neighborhood, rent and features.
2. Schedule a visit (COP $49,000 fee, often credited toward first month).
3. Review all legal documentation directly on the platform.
4. Submit your rental offer (start date, term, conditions).
5. Negotiate if needed (counter-offers supported).
6. Sign the lease agreement + key handover with legal backing.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need a co-signer / guarantor?**  
Depends on the owner. Many accept a deposit + clean credit history. Clearly stated per listing.

**Can I bring pets?**  
Each property has an explicit pet policy (see the listing).

**What does the contract include?**  
Standard Colombian residential lease + inventory annex, house rules, and clearances.

**Is there a purchase option at the end?**  
In some cases yes. Ask the owner directly through the platform.
