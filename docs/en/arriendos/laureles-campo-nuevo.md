# Rentals in Laureles and Campo Nuevo, Medellín

Laureles and Campo Nuevo are two of the most sought-after areas for rentals in Medellín thanks to the perfect balance of tradition, safety, services and quality of life.

## Laureles (including "Lauret" style properties)

Laureles is Medellín’s classic neighborhood: trees, parks, cafés, restaurants and a consolidated community. Very popular with families and foreign professionals who want to live “like locals”.

**Advantages of renting in Laureles:**
- Strong appreciation and constant demand.
- Outstanding food and cultural scene within walking distance.
- Great connections to El Poblado, Envigado and downtown.
- Lower crime index than many other areas.

Our highlighted property in the area (Casa Lauret) offers:
- 4 bedrooms + 3 bathrooms
- Private garden and BBQ zone
- 2 parking spaces
- Full legal documentation (updated CLYT + clearances)

Perfect for families or executives looking for space and tranquility without being far from everything.

## Campo Nuevo

Emerging area with excellent value for money. Modern apartments, good infrastructure and easy access via the 80 to Laureles / El Poblado.

**Advantages of renting in Campo Nuevo:**
- More accessible prices than Laureles or El Poblado.
- Newer or well-remodeled buildings with solid amenities.
- Sustained growth in the sector.
- Less traffic than central zones during peak hours.

Our Campo Nuevo property:
- 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms
- Parking + storage
- 24-hour reception
- Competitive rent

Ideal for young professionals, couples or as a first rental home.
