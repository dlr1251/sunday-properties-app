# Manage visits

Schedule visits as a buyer and manage requests as a seller.

## Prerequisites

- Verified profile (required to schedule).
- Property published with availability configured (to schedule).

---

## For buyers: schedule a visit

### 1. Find the property

Search the catalog, filter by location, price, type, bedrooms, area. Open the detail and ensure it’s published and has visits available.

### 2. Open the scheduling modal

On the property page, use "Schedule visit". The modal opens with calendar and time slots.

### 3. Choose date and time

- Browse months. Only available dates can be selected (from `property_availability` and `blocked_dates`).
- Typical rules: minimum notice (e.g. 24 h), no same-day or past dates.
- After choosing a date, pick a time from the offered slots.

### 4. Accept NDA and pay

- Read and accept the confidentiality agreement (NDA). Required.
- Pay the visit fee (e.g. 49,000 COP) via the available method (card, etc.).

### 5. Confirmation

Receive confirmation by email and in the app. The visit appears under "My visits" with status "pending" until the seller confirms.

### 6. Attend and feedback

Show up at the agreed date and time. After the visit, you can submit feedback and rating. Per business rules, this may unlock additional property documents.

---

## For sellers: manage requests

### 1. Receive the request

You get an email and in-app notification. Details include: property, buyer, requested date and time, contact info.

### 2. Review in the dashboard

Under "Visits" or "Pending visits" you see the requests. Check the buyer’s profile, verification, and that the date/time fits your availability.

### 3. Choose an action

- **Confirm**: Accept date and time. The buyer receives confirmation.
- **Reject**: Optionally add a reason. The buyer is notified.
- **Reschedule**: Propose a different date or time. The buyer can accept or decline.

### 4. Coordination and completion

After confirming, coordinate logistics (address, access). After the visit, add internal notes if needed. The visitor can leave feedback and rating.

### 5. Document unlock

If configured, completing the visit and possibly receiving feedback unlocks additional documents for the visitor.

---

## Result

- **Buyer**: Visit scheduled, confirmed, and optionally completed with feedback; possible access to more documents.
- **Seller**: Requests confirmed, rejected, or rescheduled; visit history per property.
